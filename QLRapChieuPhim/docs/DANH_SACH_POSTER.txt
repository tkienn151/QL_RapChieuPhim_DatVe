1. Thu muc chua anh tinh tich hop san trong du an:
   QLRapChieuPhim/src/main/resources/static/images/
   - no-poster.svg             (Anh du phong mac dinh khi phim khong co poster hoac anh loi)
   - posters/default-poster.jpg
   - posters/hero-banner.jpg   (Anh nen banner trang chu)
   - posters/phim01.jpg .. phim20.jpg  (20 anh poster phim mau di kem)

2. Thu muc chua anh upload thuc te khi chay ung dung:
   QLRapChieuPhim/uploads/posters/
   (WebConfig anh xa URL /images/posters/** uu tien doc file:uploads/posters/
    truoc khi tim trong classpath:/static/images/posters/.)

4. Ghi chu hien thi & upload:
   - Truong Poster trong bang Phim co the luu:
     + Ten tep anh cuc bo (vi du: phim01.jpg hoac ten anh upload UUID.jpg) -> /images/posters/<Poster>
     + Hoac duong dan bat dau bang http://, https:// hoac / -> hien thi truc tiep.
     + Hoac bo trong -> tu dong hien thi /images/no-poster.svg.
   - Khi quan tri vien upload anh moi trong trang /admin/phim/them hoac /admin/phim/sua/{id},
     he thong xem truoc (preview) ngay tren trinh duyet va luu tep vao QLRapChieuPhim/uploads/posters/.
